Research development

From inspecting existing explanations to adapting and developing patch-based explanation methods.

01

Overview

Can a patch-based explanation framework transfer across retinal imaging modalities? This study evaluates EMD-Corr, which uses Earth Mover’s Distance and patch-level re-ranking, on fundus and OCT images. It examines classification performance alongside the visual correspondences supporting predictions.

02

My role

  • Adapted and evaluated EMD-Corr across two ophthalmic imaging modalities.
  • Developed Discriminative Patch Weighting (DPW) using Fisher Discriminative Scores to weight discriminative patch features.
  • Conducted ablation experiments covering domain adaptation, classifier choice, preprocessing, patch-grid resolution, and DPW.

03

Data

  • EyePACS fundus subset: 3,568 images covering five diabetic retinopathy classes.
  • OCT2017 subset: 3,200 images across CNV, DME, Drusen, and Normal.

04

Approach

  • Compared ResNet50 softmax classification, k-nearest neighbors using 2,048-dimensional global average-pooled features, and EMD-Corr using spatial features from layer4.
  • Compared 7 × 7 and 14 × 14 patch grids and evaluated the DPW strategy.
  • Examined how domain-specific fine-tuning affects classification and patch matching.

05

Results

Domain adaptation substantially improved performance in both modalities. Patch correspondences provided visual evidence that could be examined alongside predictions. The best reported values come from the study’s configuration experiments; they are not comparisons against the other portfolio projects.

06

Limitations & next steps

  • Expert lesion annotations were unavailable, so the clinical correctness of patch localization could not be quantitatively established.
  • Visual correspondences alone do not establish explanation faithfulness or clinical reliability.

Investigate expert-annotated Vietnamese retinal datasets and clinician evaluation of explanation usefulness.
