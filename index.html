"I believe AI should not just predict — it should explain, so clinicians can truly trust it." 🌸

Background & Research

I'm a final-year B.S. student in Information Technology at Thang Long University, Hanoi, graduating July 2026 with GPA 3.2+/4.0 and a perfect thesis score of 10/10 on deep learning for diabetic retinopathy diagnosis.

My work spans medical image classification, Explainable AI (XAI), and prototype-based reasoning. I collaborate internationally with the University of Cincinnati and UTEP (USA), and work closely with ophthalmologists at Da Nang Eye Hospital and Viet Duc Hospital, Hanoi.

I presented first-authored papers at three Scopus-indexed international venues within two years as an undergraduate, and received the Special Excellence Award (Above 1st Place) at Thang Long University's research competition.
