04 / ABOUT

A little bit
about me.

I’m (Thanh) Minh Tam Nguyen. I have completed my studies in Information Technology at Thang Long University and expect to receive my bachelor’s degree in 2026. I am a member of its AI in Medicine Laboratory, led by Assoc. Prof. Nguyen Hoang Phuong. My research focuses on medical image classification and explainable AI, particularly for MRI, fundus, and OCT images.

I’ve presented my work at IFSA–NAFIPS 2025, AICI 2026, and ICTIS 2026. I’ve also met with hospital leaders to discuss and demonstrate our research at Viet Duc Hospital, Hanoi. I contributed to part of the national research project KC-4.0-18/19-25, which includes AI assisted breast cancer classification from mammograms.
